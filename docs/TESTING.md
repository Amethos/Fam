# Testing stage 1 (you on iPhone, Mom on Samsung)

Tip: do this the first time on a video call, or with you and a friend's Android
phone, before Mom tries it on her own.

## You (iPhone)

1. Open `https://<project-id>.web.app` in **Safari**. It asks **Who are you?**: tap **I help someone with their phone**.
   (Already have an account? Tap **I already have an account**.) A short 4-page story plays:
   tap **Next** through it, then **Let's set it up**.
2. Make your account: your first name, email and a password.
3. Setup walks you through the rest, one step at a time:
   - **Home screen:** tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open My Tech Kid from your home screen and sign in.
   - **Alerts:** tap **Turn on alerts** and allow notifications.
   - **Family name:** for example "Smith Family" or "Team Blue".
   - **Your photo:** take a selfie (or tap **Later**). Mom sees it on her big button and when linking.
   - **Add someone you help:** continue below.

## Link Mom's phone

1. In My Tech Kid, tap **Add someone**, pick **Your parent**, type what you call her (e.g. "Mom"), tap **Create invite link**.
2. Tap **Share link** and send it to Mom by text message. Stay on that screen.
   - Together in person? Tap **With Mom now? Show a code to scan** instead. On Mom's phone, open the
     **Camera**, point it at the code and tap the link that pops up. Then carry on from step 3 (where the
     steps say "the link", scan the code again).
3. On Mom's phone: tap the link → **Download My Tech Kid** → open the download → **Install** → **Open**.
   - Samsung may ask to allow Chrome or Samsung Internet to **install unknown apps**: allow it.
   - Samsung may warn the app is unknown (because it's not from the Play Store): choose **Install anyway**.
4. The app opens straight to the invite (the download copied it; Android may say "pasted from your
   clipboard"). If it shows the welcome screen instead, go back to Messages and tap the link again.
   If the app is already installed, tapping the link → **Open my invite** goes straight there.
5. Mom sees your photo, "Your child" and "Link with *your name*?" → **I know *name*, continue**.
6. Call Mom. You both see three pictures (e.g. Apple, Bicycle, Moon). If they match, **both** tap **They match**.
7. Your screen says "Mom is linked!" (with confetti). Stay on that screen and on the call.
8. **Practice run:** Mom's phone says "This is your help button. Let's try it once together." She taps the big button.
   Your phone buzzes and shows **Practice run: Mom just tried the help button**. Tap **Got it**.
9. Mom's phone says "*Your name* got it!" and offers **One more thing, while *you* are on the phone**:
   **Set it up now** turns on tapping (Installed apps → My Tech Kid → switch on; if Samsung says
   "Restricted setting", follow the three steps it shows), or **Later**. Then she taps **OK, got it**, then
   **Allow** when asked about notifications.
   Her home screen appears and the big button bounces in.

- **Someone who wants help, on their own:** on an Android phone, open the website → **I want help with my
  phone** → download. In the app (no link), **Text someone who can help** opens Messages with a ready-made
  text; the link in it skips "Who are you?" for the helper.

## Try the features

- **Help button:** Mom taps **Tap for help from *you***. Her phone asks "What do you need help with?" with a big
  microphone. Tap **Just ask *you* to call me**. You should get an alert. Tap **I'm on it**:
  Mom's screen says you're coming and she gets a notification. When done, tap **All fixed**.
- **Voice message:** Mom taps Tap for help, then the microphone. The first time, her phone asks about the
  microphone: tap **While using the app**. She sees a red "*you* will hear this" bar; she says something
  and stops talking (it sends after 4 seconds of quiet) or taps **Send**. Your alert says
  **Mom needs help · voice message** and never shows her words. In the app, tap **▶ Play Mom's message**.
  A few seconds later her words may appear under it (Android 13 or newer, when the phone can do speech
  recognition offline); both her phone and yours show them. Also try: tapping nothing for 30 seconds
  sends the request without a message.
- **See Mom's screen:** after you tap **I'm on it**, Android's "share your screen" prompt comes up on Mom's
  phone by itself (once, a couple of seconds later) and she taps **Start**. If she cancels, **Show *you* my
  screen** is still there. Mom's screen opens on your phone by itself (or tap the alert → **Watch Mom's screen**). Her phone shows *you* can see her screen with a
  big **Stop** button (also in her notifications). Try a banking app: it should show black. Either side tapping
  **Stop** ends it on both phones.
- **Tap for Mom:** her Start already allows tapping, so once her switch is on you can tap as soon as her screen shows. If the switch is still off, tap the menu button (top right), then **Ask to tap**.
  - The first time, her phone needs a one-time switch. A card on your screen lists what to read to her, and
    moves along as she goes. Her app shows the same steps in pictures: she taps **Agree and show me where**,
    then on a Samsung **Installed apps** (other phones: **Downloaded apps**), **My Tech Kid: let family tap for
    me**, turns it on → **Allow**. Your card turns green by itself once the switch is on.
  - If Android says **Restricted setting**, she comes back to My Tech Kid, which now shows how to fix it:
    **Take me there** → **⋮** (top right) → **Allow restricted settings**, then **Turn it on** again. (Only for
    apps installed from a link; it goes away once we're on Google Play.) **Not now** on her phone counts as no.
  - Tapping turns on by itself (no second question). A dark bar with your
    name and **Stop** stays at the top of her screen.
  - On your phone, tap, press and hold, or swipe on her screen; use her phone's buttons (Recent apps, Home,
    Back) in the bar along the bottom, and **Pull down alerts** in the menu. Each tap shows as an amber dot on her phone. You can't tap her **Stop** yourself.
  - **Stop** on her bar, in her notification, in her app, or on your page ends seeing and tapping at once.
  - Turn her phone sideways: the picture shows it sideways with black bars, and taps should still land
    where you aim.
  - Reload your page while watching: her screen should come back by itself within a few seconds. Tapping
    needs her **Yes** again.
  - A share ends on its own after an hour, and right away if the connection drops for good.
    Mom taps **Show my screen** again to carry on.
- **Ring Mom's phone:** put Mom's phone on silent somewhere nearby. On your dashboard, tap **Ring Mom's phone**.
  It should play loudly for 30 seconds and say *you* are ringing it. Mom taps **Found it** (on the screen or in the
  notification) to stop it. Her normal volume should come back afterwards.
- **Phone type:** Mom's card on your dashboard should show her phone (for example "Samsung SM-S911U · Android 14")
  and say she can show you her screen from the app.
- **Scam check (typed):** Mom taps **Is this a scam?**, types or pastes a message, taps **Send**.
  You get an alert → **Answer** → pick Scam / Not sure / Safe, add a note → **Send to Mom**.
  Mom sees your answer.
- **Scam check (shared):** In Samsung Messages, Mom presses and holds a text → **Share** → **My Tech Kid**.

## Updating Mom's app

When a new version is published, Mom's home screen shows **A new My Tech Kid is ready** with an **Update** button
(it checks each time she opens the app).

1. Mom taps **Update**, waits a few seconds, then taps **Update** again on Android's screen.
2. **The very first time only**, Samsung says it can't install from this source: tap **Settings**, switch on
   **Allow from this source**, then tap Back. Best done while you're on the phone with her.
3. When it says the app is updated, tap **Open**.

Phones with a version from before this feature need one last manual install from the download link.

## Things to try breaking (good for safety)

- Open the same invite link on a **second** Android phone after Mom used it → it should refuse.
- Tap **They don't match — stop** on either phone → nothing gets linked.
- Wait more than an hour before using an invite → it should say it expired.
- After Mom is linked, send her a new invite → her phone should say it's already linked.
- On your dashboard, tap **Remove this phone** under Mom → her app goes back to the welcome screen.
  Send her a new invite to link her again.

## What's not in this version yet

Talking in the app and drawing arrows on her screen come later. For now, talk on a regular phone call
while you see and tap her screen. There's no relay server yet, so some strict work or hotel Wi-Fi may not connect.
